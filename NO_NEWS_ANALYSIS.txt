COMPREHENSIVE ANALYSIS: Why 41 Out of 100 Events Got Zero Ticker News from Polygon
================================================================================

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
================================================================================
Of the 100 selected events, 41 (41%) have NO ticker-specific news in the Polygon
API within the search window (t0-3h to t0+1h). This is a surprisingly high ratio
that warrants investigation.

KEY FINDINGS:
1. NFLX, PLTR, QQQ, and XLK are the most affected (66-100% no-news rate)
2. Most no-news events occur at 14:00 UTC (2pm ET / 9:30am EST opening bell)
3. Friday and Thursday have higher no-news rates (56-64%) than other days
4. No-news events actually have STRONGER average returns (192.6 bp vs 169.9 bp)
5. The 4-hour news window may be appropriate, but API coverage is uneven

5. NEWS WINDOW ANALYSIS (t0-3h to t0+1h)
────────────────────────────────────────

Current Window: 4 hours (3 hours before event, 1 hour after)

Rationale Check:
  - 3 hours before: Captures material news released that day
  - 1 hour after: Captures immediate reactions/press releases after event
  - 4 hours total: Reasonable for intraday event windows

Effectiveness:
  - Captures news for 59/100 events (59.0%)
  - Misses news for 41/100 events (41.0%)

Is 4 hours too narrow?
  POSSIBLY YES for some cases:

  1. Gap Events (Overnight News):
     - 14:00 UTC opening bell events (31 no-news) may have caused by:
       - Overnight earnings releases (published 8pm-9pm prior day)
       - Overnight international news (Asian/European markets)
       - Pre-market analyst notes
     - These would be BEFORE t0-3h window
     - Solution: Extend lookback to 6-12 hours for opening events

  2. Post-Market Events:
     - 19:00+ UTC events (after-hours) typically have low news
     - News may come AFTER close (8pm ET typical analyst releases)
     - Solution: Extend lookahead to 3-4 hours for after-hours

  3. Tick-Driven Events (No News):
     - Options expiry, rebalancing, gamma hedging
     - These are REAL events with NO news catalyst
     - Solution: Accept these as "no news" - it's correct


Polygon API Coverage Hypothesis:
  - Some tickers (XLK, PLTR, NFLX, QQQ) have structurally lower coverage
  - Polygon may not index all news sources equally
  - Could be missing regional/specialized news for lower-coverage tickers
  - Can't fix without access to multiple news APIs


CONCLUSIONS
================================================================================

1. THE MAIN ISSUE: Opening Bell Concentration (75% of no-news at 14:00 UTC)
   - 31 out of 41 no-news events occur exactly at 10am ET (market open)
   - These are likely technical/momentum-driven spikes, not news-driven
   - This is probably CORRECT - market open doesn't always have news catalyst
   - These are valid "no news" events for your ML classifier

2. TICKER COVERAGE BIAS: ETFs and low-coverage stocks are problematic
   - XLK (100%), NFLX (71%), PLTR (67%), QQQ (67%) have structural issues
   - Mega-caps (MSFT 9%, AAPL 20%) have excellent coverage
   - This reflects Polygon's coverage, not your data quality

3. THE 4-HOUR WINDOW IS REASONABLE
   - For regular trading hours: 3h lookback + 1h lookahead is appropriate
   - For opening bell events: These should look earlier (extend to 6-12h back)
   - For after-hours events: These should look later (extend to 3-4h after)

4. NO-NEWS EVENTS ARE REAL AND VALUABLE
   - Average return 192.6 bp vs 169.9 bp for news events
   - These are likely technical/momentum moves, rebalancing, gamma events
   - Your classifier SHOULD learn to distinguish news-driven vs non-news moves
   - This is a genuine market regime (important for trading)

5. RECOMMENDATIONS FOR NEXT STEPS:
   a) Keep all 41 no-news events in training set
   b) Create a feature: "is_opening_bell" (14:00 UTC) to help classifier
   c) Create a feature: "ticker_news_coverage" (categorical for high/low)
   d) Consider separate analysis for XLK/PLTR - may need different treatment
   e) For production: Use multiple news APIs, not just Polygon
   f) Label the 41 no-news events carefully:
      - Are they reversal/continuation driven by technicals?
      - Can your classifier predict direction without news?
