FINDING #6: NEWS WINDOW ANALYSIS (4 hours adequate?)
================================================================================

Current Window: t0-3h to t0+1h (4 hours total)

Window Breakdown:
  - Lookback: 3 hours before event
  - Lookahead: 1 hour after event
  - Total span: 4 hours

Effectiveness:
  - Events with news: 59/100 (59%)
  - Events without news: 41/100 (41%)

For OPENING BELL events (14:00 UTC, n=31):
  - Window starts: 11:00 am ET
  - Event occurs: 10:00 am ET ← BEFORE window!
  - Problem: Missing overnight/pre-market news
  - Solution: Extend lookback to 6-12 hours for opening events
  - Recommendation: YES, window is too narrow for gaps

For AFTERNOON events (15:00-18:00 UTC, n=4):
  - Window appropriately covers event
  - Good coverage of relevant news
  - Assessment: WINDOW IS ADEQUATE

For AFTER-HOURS events (19:00+ UTC, n=3):
  - Window ends: 1 hour after event
  - News typically releases 8pm+ ET (00:00+ UTC)
  - Problem: Missing post-market analyst releases
  - Solution: Extend lookahead to 3-4 hours
  - Average return: 403.6 bp (STRONG, likely news-driven)
  - Recommendation: YES, window too narrow for after-hours

Overall Assessment:
  ✓ Window is GOOD for regular trading hours (15:00-18:00 UTC)
  ✗ Window is TOO NARROW for opening-bell events (need -6h to 0h)
  ✗ Window is TOO NARROW for after-hours events (need +3-4h lookahead)

Recommendation: Use adaptive window based on event time:
  - 06:00 UTC lookback + 01:00 UTC lookahead = 07:00 total (for opening-bell)
  - 03:00 UTC lookback + 01:00 UTC lookahead = 04:00 total (for regular hours)
  - 03:00 UTC lookback + 04:00 UTC lookahead = 07:00 total (for after-hours)
